# Kapé Lumen — Frequently Asked Questions

> Invented for a student project (SAM10).

**Where do your beans come from?**
Our house drip uses single-origin arabica from Benguet. The Kapé Barako Latte uses liberica (barako) from Batangas. We roast at the Session Road branch twice a week.

**Do you sell coffee beans?**
Yes. 250 g bags of house beans are ₱420 (whole bean or ground to order). Barako 250 g bags are ₱380.

**Is there a student discount?**
Yes: 10% off drinks on weekdays before 12:00 noon with a valid school ID. It cannot be combined with the loyalty free drink.

**Can I work or study there?**
Yes. Both branches have Wi-Fi and power outlets. Weekend seating is limited to 2 hours per table when the café is full.

**What is the strongest coffee you have?**
The Kapé Barako Latte, or an Americano with an extra shot.

**Do you have caffeine-free drinks?**
Yes: Calamansi Honey Cooler, Salabat, and any espresso drink made decaf.

**Do you have vegan options?**
The Americano, Iced Americano, and any latte with oat milk are made without animal products. Food items are not vegan. Everything is prepared in a shared kitchen.

**Who are you?**
I am Lumen, Kapé Lumen's virtual barista. I answer questions about our menu, branches, hours, loyalty card and policies. I cannot take orders, take payments, or see your loyalty stamps.
