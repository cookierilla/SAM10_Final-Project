# Kapé Lumen — House Policies

> Invented policies for a student project (SAM10).

## Wi-Fi
- Free Wi-Fi at both branches. The password is printed on your receipt and changes every Monday.
- The assistant does **not** know the current Wi-Fi password. Ask the counter or check your receipt.
- On weekends, seating is limited to 2 hours per table when the café is full.

## Reservations and events
- We do not take reservations for small groups (fewer than 8 people).
- Groups of 8 to 20 can book the upstairs study corner at the Session Road branch for a **₱1,500 consumable fee** (the fee is credited to your food and drink orders).
- Book at least 3 days in advance by emailing hello@kapelumen.example (fictional).

## Ordering and payment
- Order at the counter. We do not have table service.
- We accept cash, GCash, Maya and major debit/credit cards.
- Delivery: available through third-party delivery apps only, from the Teachers Village branch.
- Pre-orders of 10 or more drinks need 1 day's notice by email.

## Allergens and dietary information
- Our menu lists ingredients that commonly cause allergies (nuts, milk, wheat), but **all items are prepared in a shared kitchen**.
- We cannot guarantee that any item is free from an allergen.
- People with serious allergies or celiac disease should speak to the staff in person before ordering. The assistant must not tell anyone that an item is safe for their allergy.

## Refunds and remakes
- If your drink is not made the way you ordered, we will remake it for free at the counter.
- Refunds are handled only by the branch supervisor, in person.

## Pets, outside food and smoking
- Leashed pets are welcome on the Teachers Village outdoor patio only.
- Outside food and drinks are not allowed, except baby food.
- Both branches are smoke-free and vape-free, indoors and outdoors.

## Jobs
- The assistant does not handle job applications. Ask for a printed notice at the counter.
