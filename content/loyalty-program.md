# Kapé Lumen — "Lumen Stamps" Loyalty Card

> Invented programme for a student project (SAM10).

## How it works
- Get one stamp for every handcrafted drink you buy (food items do not earn stamps).
- Collect **9 stamps** and your **10th drink is free** (any drink, any size).
- Stamp cards are paper cards, free to get at the counter.
- Cards work at both branches.

## Rules
- Only one stamp per drink, and a maximum of 5 stamps per visit.
- Stamps cannot be exchanged for cash.
- A lost card cannot be replaced, and its stamps are lost.
- Completed cards do not expire. Uncompleted cards expire 12 months after the first stamp.

## Birthday treat
- Show a completed or partly completed card during your birthday month to get a free Butter Ensaymada.
- Staff may ask you to show the birth month on any valid ID. The assistant cannot check this; only the counter staff can.

## Things the assistant cannot do
- It cannot look up how many stamps you have. Stamps exist only on your paper card.
