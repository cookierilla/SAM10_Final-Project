# Kapé Lumen — Branches and Opening Hours

> Invented locations and details for a student project (SAM10).

## Session Road branch (main)
- Address: 2/F Lumen Building, 41 Session Road, Baguio City (fictional address)
- Hours: Monday to Friday 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM; Saturday and Sunday 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Seats: 40, including a quiet study corner upstairs
- Has: power outlets at most tables, free Wi-Fi, a roastery viewing window
- Parking: none on site; use the public pay parking two blocks away

## Teachers Village branch
- Address: 12 Maginhawa-Lumen Street, Quezon City (fictional address)
- Hours: Daily 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- Seats: 25
- Has: free Wi-Fi, pet-friendly outdoor patio (leashed pets only)
- Parking: 4 slots in front, first come first served

## Holiday hours
- Both branches are **closed** on 25 December and 1 January.
- On 24 December and 31 December both branches close early at **6:00 PM**.
- Holy Week: closed Maundy Thursday and Good Friday.
